## Binding gates

- **Transport/session boundary:** `packages/desktop/src/main.ts:23–59` forwards `/api/*` only and pumps the local event channel. Remote transport stays in main; access/refresh/pending tokens and cookie storage never cross to renderer. Origin/account identity must scope session material, continuations, retries, streams and approvals. `ConnectionUrl` permits HTTP(S) origins only: no credentials, paths, queries or fragments; `remote.ts` refuses redirects. `postLibrary` requires allowed browser Origin for mutations in none mode; prototype reachability proves neither account identity nor deployment origin policy.
- **Authentication contract limits:** `getAuthLogin` GET `/v1/auth/login`, `getAuthCallback` GET `/v1/auth/callback`, `postAuthRefresh` POST `/v1/auth/refresh`, `postAuthLogout` POST `/v1/auth/logout`, `getMe` GET `/v1/me` exist but have unconstrained success schemas here; no complete SSO/refresh/account adapter follows from route presence. `LocalSession` contains no owner identity. `Problem.mfa=reauth_required` requires `postAuthLocal`, then owner-verified replay of the identical pending method/path/body/idempotency key once; cancel/account mismatch/repeated requirement must stop. `postMeDeletion` POST `/v1/me/deletion` and `postMeExport` POST `/v1/me/export` exist, but their request bodies are not specified; the draft's empty body is not canonical proof.
